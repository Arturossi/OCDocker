OCDocker.OCScore.Optimization.OptunaSearchSpace module¶

Centralized Optuna search-space definitions for staged OCScore optimization.

Edit the dataclasses in this module to expand or restrict hyperparameter search spaces without modifying sampler logic in StagedOptuna.py.
